# Provenance

Source manuscript: **Training Scores for Conditional Audits: Overlap Weighting and Power under Confounding**, supplied as `62221_Training_Scores_for_Cond (4).pdf`.

The code in this repository was newly written on 3 November 2026 from the described train/test procedure and Equations 6–8. It is not the original simulation implementation. The README's 61.6% and 79.4% are attributed paper results. The illustrative generator is newly designed and cannot reproduce those results.

Train strata and score orientation must be fixed without test-label selection. Exchangeability and independence are assumptions, not properties certified by the implementation. No clinical or surveillance dataset is included.
